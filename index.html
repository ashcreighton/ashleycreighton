Ashley Creighton is a songwriter, mult-instrumentalist, and singer who arranged and recorded a large portion of her debut EP from her home in Phoenix, Arizona. She is inspired by any song that is genuinely infectious, and is a lifelong student of the fine and expansive art of pop music.

As an entertainer and skillful song interpreter, Ashley is available for public and private events and will take you on a journey of rediscovering your favorite songs — from Patsy Cline, to Van Morrison, to Michael Jackson. Her one-of-a-kind blend of fine-tuned, intricate guitar stylings and soulful, intimate vocals will create musical moments that are deeply familiar and also strikingly original.
